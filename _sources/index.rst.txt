==============
Leonhard Euler
==============

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Leonhard Euler <euler>` (1707–1783), the Swiss mathematician whose 1765 memoir discovered the Euler line connecting the orthocenter, centroid, and circumcenter of every triangle.
